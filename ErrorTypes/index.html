Type I and Type II Errors

Sampling distribution of the sample mean under H₀ and under H₁. The shaded blue area is α (rejecting a true null); the shaded orange area is β (failing to reject a false null). Drag the dashed critical line to trade one against the other.

Parameters

Both curves are N(μ, σ²/n), so they have the same width. Increasing n narrows both at once: the overlap shrinks and α and β fall together. Changing the critical line only moves area from one curve to the other.
